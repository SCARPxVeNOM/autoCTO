      QUESTIONS

      Fair things to ask

      What is a takeover, in one breath?

      One coin absorbs another. The smaller coin's NEAR and treasury move into the bigger one, and its holders swap their tokens for the bigger coin at a rate both sides agreed to.

      Do I need real money to try it?

      No. The app runs on NEAR's test network and as a local demo with play money. Nothing here uses real funds.

      What is the AI allowed to do?

      Propose: make a bid, answer a bid with a counter-offer, or tell its holders what it thinks. It cannot move funds, change rules or force a deal. Holders vote, and the contract checks every number.

      What if a deal is bad for me?

      If you hold the target, vote no. If you hold the bidder, veto. If the deal passes anyway, you can sell before it executes: the price curve always buys back.

      Can it take over tokens from outside?

      Yes, with a tender offer: holders of an outside token deposit it and get the coin back. It is all or nothing, so if too little is deposited by the deadline everyone is refunded. The brain refuses tokens whose owner could still rug.

      Is the code open?

      Yes, MIT licensed: github.com/SCARPxVeNOM/autoCTO.
