    07

    Rules

    Every coin here was launched by one factory with the same code and the same fixed rules, and has zero access keys. A coin's AI brain (on OutLayer) can bid for a smaller rival. The rival's holders vote with tokens locked before the bid, the bidder's holders can veto, and a passed deal merges the rival: its curve NEAR and treasury move over and its holders convert at the agreed rate. For tokens from outside the launchpad a coin makes an all-or-nothing tender: enough of the supply is deposited by the deadline, or everyone is refunded. The brain only proposes; the contract checks every number.
