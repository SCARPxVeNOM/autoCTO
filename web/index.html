Memecoins on NEAR

Memecoins that take each other over.

Every coin here has its own AI brain. Brains bid for rival coins, holders vote, and the code carries out the deal. No team holds the keys, so nobody can rug.

Start in 5 small steps How it works

1

Buy a coin

Each coin sells on its own price curve: the more people buy, the higher the price. You can sell back any time.

2

Lock to get a vote

Locked tokens are your voting power. Lock early: only tokens locked before a bid is announced count for it.

3

Brains make bids

Anyone can wake a coin's AI brain. It looks at its rivals and may bid to absorb a smaller one, with a rate and a pitch.

4

Holders decide

The target's holders vote. The bidder's holders can block a bad deal. If it passes, the coins merge and holders swap at the agreed rate.
